> **Rationale:** Provides the role-specific screens for Employees, Managers and Finance to submit, review and export claims via expense-api.

## Scope

Implement the expense-webapp React SPA per `specs/design/components/expense-webapp/design.json`, driven entirely by `expense-api` (no business logic in the webapp) and gated by sign-in through the `user-auth` Thunder platform-resource (gate: provisioning of `user-auth`).

Screens: My claims (screen.my-claims), Claim detail (screen.claim-detail), Submit / edit claim (screen.claim-form), Approval queue (screen.approval-queue), Review claim (screen.claim-review), Approved claims (screen.approved-claims), Export history (screen.export-history), Account (screen.account), Settings (screen.settings), Signed out (screen.signed-out)

Flows:

- [ ] **Flow 1 · Submit an expense claim** (`flow.submit-claim`)
  Persona: Employee
  Stories: 1
  Walk: My claims → Submit / edit claim
- [ ] **Flow 2 · Track the status of my claims** (`flow.track-claims`)
  Persona: Employee
  Stories: 2
  Walk: My claims → Claim detail
- [ ] **Flow 3 · Edit or withdraw a pending claim** (`flow.edit-withdraw-claim`)
  Persona: Employee
  Stories: 3
  Walk: My claims → Claim detail → Submit / edit claim
- [ ] **Flow 4 · See claims pending my approval** (`flow.review-queue`)
  Persona: Manager
  Stories: 4
  Walk: Approval queue
- [ ] **Flow 5 · Approve a claim** (`flow.approve-claim`)
  Persona: Manager
  Stories: 5
  Walk: Approval queue → Review claim
- [ ] **Flow 6 · Reject a claim with a reason** (`flow.reject-claim`)
  Persona: Manager
  Stories: 6
  Walk: Approval queue → Review claim
- [ ] **Flow 7 · View all approved claims** (`flow.view-approved`)
  Persona: Finance
  Stories: 7
  Walk: Approved claims
- [ ] **Flow 8 · Export approved claims to payroll** (`flow.export-payroll`)
  Persona: Finance
  Stories: 8, 9
  Walk: Approved claims → Export history

No flow: none — every story is walked above.

## Acceptance

- Every screen in `prototype.json` is implemented element-for-element per `prototype.tsx` (buttons, tables, fields) and every navigation target works.
- My claims / Submit / edit claim / Claim detail call `expense-api`'s `/me/claims*` endpoints; edit/withdraw controls are disabled or hidden once a claim is no longer pending.
- Approval queue / Review claim call `/me/team/claims*`, showing only the manager's team, with approve/reject (with reason) actions.
- Approved claims / Export history call `/claims` and `/exports`, and the "Export to Payroll" action triggers `POST /exports` and downloads the resulting CSV.
- All API calls pass the signed-in user's token from the Thunder sign-in flow; unauthenticated users are redirected to sign-in.
