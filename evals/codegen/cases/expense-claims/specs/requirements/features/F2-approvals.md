# Approvals

## Purpose

A manager reviews the pending claims of their employees and approves or rejects each one.

Needs: F1.

## User Stories

- F2.1 As a Manager, I want to see a list of claims pending my approval, so that I can act on them.
- F2.2 As a Manager, I want to approve a claim, so that it becomes eligible for payroll export.
- F2.3 As a Manager, I want to reject a claim with a reason, so that the employee understands why it was not approved.

## Decisions

- A Manager only sees claims from employees assigned to them. *assumed*
