# Front desk

## Purpose
A receptionist books, reschedules and cancels for patients who call or walk in, and checks them in on arrival.

Needs: F1.

## User Stories
- F3.1 As a receptionist, I want to book, reschedule or cancel an appointment for a patient, so that patients who call or walk in are served.
- F3.2 As a receptionist, I want to check in a patient on arrival, so that the doctor knows they are present.

## Decisions
- Check-in is allowed only on the day of the appointment. *assumed*
