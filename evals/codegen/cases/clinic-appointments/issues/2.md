> **Rationale:** Build-order edge to clinic-api; gated on user-auth (thunder-app) provisioning.

## Scope
Implement the React SPA per specs/design/components/clinic-webapp/design.json and its prototype (prototype.json, prototype.tsx), calling clinic-api (build-order dependency) and signing in via Thunder (user-auth platform-resource gate, provisioned by the platform). Screens gated by caller scopes.

Screens: Browse slots (screen.slots), My appointments (screen.appointments), Appointment detail (screen.appointment-detail), My availability (screen.my-slots), My day (screen.schedule), Visit note (screen.visit-note), Front desk (screen.front-desk), Account (screen.account), Settings (screen.settings), Signed out (screen.signed-out)

Flows:

- [ ] **Flow 1 · Publish availability** (`flow.publish-slots`)
  Persona: Doctor
  Stories: 1
  Walk: My availability
- [ ] **Flow 2 · Remove an unbooked slot** (`flow.remove-slot`)
  Persona: Doctor
  Stories: 2

- [ ] **Flow 3 · Browse and book a slot** (`flow.browse-and-book`)
  Persona: Patient
  Stories: 3, 4
  Walk: Browse slots
- [ ] **Flow 4 · Reschedule or cancel an appointment** (`flow.manage-appointments`)
  Persona: Patient

  Walk: My appointments → Appointment detail
- [ ] **Flow 5 · Book, reschedule or cancel for a patient** (`flow.front-desk-booking`)
  Persona: Receptionist

- [ ] **Flow 6 · Check in a patient** (`flow.check-in`)
  Persona: Receptionist

  Persona: Doctor

  Persona: Doctor

## Acceptance
- Every screen and flow above is implemented and walkable per the prototype.
- Unauthenticated visitors see no data; screens/nav shown only for the role's scopes.
- Build and verify steps of pinned skills pass.
