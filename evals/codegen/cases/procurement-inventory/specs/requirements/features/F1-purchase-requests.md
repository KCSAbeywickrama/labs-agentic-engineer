# Purchase requests

## Purpose
A requester picks catalogue items, raises a purchase request and follows it to approval and receipt.

Needs: F4.

## User Stories
- F1.1 As a Requester, I want to browse the catalogue of items, so that I can choose what to request.
- F1.2 As a Requester, I want to raise a purchase request for catalogue items and quantities, so that I can obtain goods I need.
- F1.3 As a Requester, I want to see the status of my purchase requests, so that I know whether they were approved, rejected or received.
